AI enablement · 2026 – present

Turning individual experiments into organizational capability

My role sits between people, process and AI tools. I build the networks, habits and measures that help teams move from trying AI to working better with it.

Community model

AI champions network

  • Problem

    AI maturity varied widely across functions, and good ideas stayed inside the team that found them. Knowledge sharing was one of the largest recorded blockers to adoption.

  • Action

    I recruited 20 change agents from 7 SBUs. They stay in their own functions, test new workflows and bring what they learn back to the group. I then set up a quarterly cadence for documenting use cases, with a standard data set that captures the conditions for each role, from creative to developer.

  • Result

    The network has run for 6+ months. Two quarterly cycles have produced 45+ documented use cases that anyone in the organization can open.

Measurement

Measuring capability, not logins

  • Problem

    Usage numbers alone couldn't show whether people had the skills, confidence or time to use AI well.

  • Action

    I designed a company-wide survey measuring capability, readiness and capacity. To get honest, broad participation, we protected time in calendars, offered incentives, and asked champions and leaders to encourage their teams.

  • Result

    72% of employees responded. The results shaped role-level training, coaching and documentation, plus "embarrassment tax free" lunch and learns where people chose the topics.

Reported to leadership under two OKRs: Increase AI Adoption and Increase Efficiency Gains, tracked through survey scoring, token spend and delivery.

Change management

Turning a stalled internal tool into a habit

  • Problem

    26% of an internal AI tool's 123 users logged in once and never came back.

  • Action

    With technical PMs and Product Owners, I set up testing, formal requirements gathering, a rollout strategy, feedback loops and enhancement sprints, so the tool improved in step with how people used it.

  • Result

    The user base grew to 345 while one-time-only users fell to 9%. Returning users increased their usage by 48%.

Approach

How I work

Start with people

Adoption stalls on confidence, habits and unclear expectations more often than on the tools. I design for those first.

Measure what people can do

Logins show activity. Capability, readiness and capacity show whether work is actually changing.

Scale what works

Champions find good ideas in their teams. A shared cadence and standard documentation let other teams find and adapt them.

Move with governance

I work alongside Legal/governance and IT so guidance on responsible use and human review grows alongside enablement.
